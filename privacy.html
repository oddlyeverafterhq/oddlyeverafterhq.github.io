Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the Oddly Ever After website (oddlyeverafterhq.github.io) and Oddly Ever After Uploader, the in-house tool we use to publish videos to our YouTube channel. Questions: oddlyeverafterhq@gmail.com.

1. We use YouTube API Services

Oddly Ever After Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Who uses the Uploader

The Uploader is an internal tool. Its only user is the Oddly Ever After team, signed in with our own Google account to manage our own channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not request or receive data from any other Google or YouTube user.

3. What data the Uploader accesses

When we authorize the Uploader with Google OAuth, it receives two permissions for our own account:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload our video files and set their title, description, tags, category, language, privacy status and scheduled publish time.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read our channel's basic details and the upload and processing status of our own videos.

It also keeps the OAuth token that Google issues, so scheduled uploads can run without signing in each time.

4. How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The data is used only to publish and check our own videos.
  • The OAuth token is stored as a private secret in our automation environment and is never published. Video files and their metadata are kept in our private workspace.
  • Our publishing workflow is automated. Its scripts may be run by an AI assistant working on our behalf (Claude, by Anthropic). The assistant handles only the data described above, and only to carry out these tasks.
  • We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • The Uploader does not let third parties serve content or ads.
  • Data received through YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Oddly Ever After Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, we delete the stored token and any data obtained through YouTube API Services within 7 days. You can also ask us to delete data by email.

6. This website

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or ads, and we do not collect personal data from visitors. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect its service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Links to YouTube take you to YouTube, where Google's policies apply.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Our website and tool are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Contact: oddlyeverafterhq@gmail.com.
