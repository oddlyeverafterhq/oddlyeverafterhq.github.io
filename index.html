What we make

  • Short vertical cartoons, under a minute each, about real events.
  • Characters and worlds built entirely in code, in a paper-cutout style.
  • Sources checked before every script. Myths are clearly labeled as myths.

Oddly Ever After Uploader

Oddly Ever After Uploader is our in-house publishing tool. It uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API v3) to:

  • upload our finished videos to our own YouTube channel, @OddlyEverAfterTV;
  • set each video's title, description, tags, language and scheduled publish time;
  • read our own channel's video list to confirm that an upload has finished processing.

The tool is used only by the Oddly Ever After team, with our own Google account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube data. See our Privacy Policy for what data it uses and how access can be revoked, and our Terms of Service.
